Welcome to TF2 Descent

A co-op dungeon crawl in the spirit of Diablo, built on Team Fortress 2.
Your party fights its way down through the depths of a mine overrun by
robot hordes.

THE TOWN
You start in town. Its stalls sell weapons, identify, heal and gamble;
the Engineer repairs and his bench crafts; the stash keeps your spare
gear. Take the cage down to the depth you choose.

THE MINE
Robots drop loot and Australium, the town's currency. Experience brings
levels, attribute points and skill ranks.

DEATH
A fallen hero can be revived: hold E beside him. Alone, you die and come
back at the level's arrival point without your gear, which waits on your
gravestone where you fell. Use the gravestone to take it back.

KEYS
E - Use: a stall, the cage, loot, a gravestone, a friend
C - Character sheet
I - Inventory
K - Skills
M - Automap
P - Party
L - Miner's lamp
4 to 9 - Skills on the skill bar
Keypad 1 to 4 - Belt
